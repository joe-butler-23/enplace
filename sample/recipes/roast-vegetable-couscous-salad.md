A colourful salad that works warm for dinner or cold in a lunchbox.

## Ingredients
- 1 courgette, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 200 g couscous
- 250 ml hot vegetable stock
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 50 g rocket
- 1 lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C and roast the courgette, pepper and onion with half the oil for 25 minutes.
2. Cover the couscous with the stock, leave for 8 minutes and fluff with a fork.
3. Mix everything together with the tomatoes, rocket, lemon juice and remaining oil.
