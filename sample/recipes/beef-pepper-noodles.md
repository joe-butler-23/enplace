Fast, glossy noodles for the night when only a hot stir-fry will do.

## Ingredients
- 250 g dried egg noodles
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 300 g beef rump steak, thinly sliced
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp brown sugar

## Method
1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, then drain.
2. Stir-fry the beef in the oil for 2 minutes, add the pepper and carrot and cook for 3 minutes more.
3. Add the noodles, spring onions, soy sauce, lime juice and sugar, then toss over the heat for 1 minute.
