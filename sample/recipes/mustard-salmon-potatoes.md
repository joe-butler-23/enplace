# Mustard salmon with crushed potatoes

![Mustard salmon with crushed potatoes](<images/mustard-salmon-potatoes.webp>)

A fresh salmon supper with crisp-edged potatoes and green beans.

*fish, weeknight*

---

- *600 g* baby potatoes
- *250 g* green beans, trimmed
- *2 tbsp* olive oil
- *4* salmon fillets
- *1 tbsp* Dijon mustard
- *1 tsp* runny honey
- *1* lemon, half juiced and half cut into wedges
- *10 g* parsley, chopped

---

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes, adding the beans for the final 4 minutes, then drain.
2. Crush the potatoes onto a tray with half the oil and bake at 220°C for 15 minutes.
3. Brush the salmon with mustard and honey, add it to the tray and bake for 10 minutes.
4. Toss the beans with the lemon juice, parsley and remaining oil, then serve with the salmon and potatoes.
