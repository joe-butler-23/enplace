A soft, not-too-sweet loaf for breakfast slices or an afternoon snack.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 80 ml vegetable oil
- 100 g light brown sugar
- 150 g plain flour
- 100 g porridge oats
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 50 ml milk

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas, then whisk in the eggs, oil, sugar and milk.
3. Fold in the flour, oats, baking powder and cinnamon.
4. Spoon into the tin and bake for 50 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
