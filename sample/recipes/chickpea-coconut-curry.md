A mild, creamy cupboard curry that is easy to sharpen with lime.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 20 g fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp medium curry powder
- 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 400 ml tin coconut milk
- 2 × 400 g tins chickpeas, drained
- 150 g spinach
- 1 lime, juiced

## Method
1. Soften the onion in the oil for 7 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger and curry powder.
2. Stir in the tomatoes, coconut milk and chickpeas, then simmer for 20 minutes.
3. Wilt in the spinach and finish with the lime juice.
