# Lemon chicken traybake

![Lemon chicken traybake](<images/lemon-chicken-traybake.webp>)

A bright, hands-off chicken dinner for a busy weeknight.

*one-tray, weeknight*

---

- *600 g* baby potatoes, halved
- *1* red onion, cut into wedges
- *2 tbsp* olive oil
- *4* chicken thighs
- *1* lemon, cut into wedges
- *2* garlic cloves, sliced
- *1 tsp* dried oregano
- *150 g* frozen peas

---

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 210°C and toss the potatoes and onion with half the oil.
2. Roast for 20 minutes, then add the chicken, lemon, garlic, oregano and remaining oil.
3. Roast for 30 minutes more, scatter over the peas and cook for a final 5 minutes.
