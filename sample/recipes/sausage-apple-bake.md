A savoury-sweet tray of sausages and roots for a cool evening.

## Ingredients
- 500 g new potatoes, halved
- 2 carrots, cut into thick slices
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 8 pork sausages
- 2 eating apples, cut into wedges
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 1 tsp dried thyme

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 210°C and roast the potatoes, carrots and onion with the oil for 20 minutes.
2. Add the sausages and roast for another 20 minutes.
3. Toss in the apples, mustard and thyme, then bake for 15 minutes until browned.
