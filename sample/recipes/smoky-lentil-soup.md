# Smoky lentil soup

![Smoky lentil soup](<images/smoky-lentil-soup.webp>)

A thick, gently spiced soup for a cold lunch or simple supper.

*soup, vegetarian*

---

- *1 tbsp* olive oil
- *1* carrot, diced
- *1* onion, diced
- *2* celery sticks, diced
- *1 tsp* smoked paprika
- *200 g* red lentils, rinsed
- *400 g* tin chopped tomatoes
- *900 ml* vegetable stock
- *1 tbsp* red wine vinegar

---

## Method
1. Soften the carrot, onion and celery in the oil for 8 minutes.
2. Stir in the paprika, lentils, tomatoes and stock, then simmer for 25 minutes.
3. Blend half the soup, stir it back into the pan and add the vinegar.
