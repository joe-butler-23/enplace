# Spinach and feta omelette

![Spinach and feta omelette](<images/spinach-feta-omelette.webp>)

A quick, savoury breakfast that is also substantial enough for lunch.

*breakfast, vegetarian*

---

- *3* eggs
- *1 tbsp* milk
- *1 tsp* olive oil
- *60 g* spinach
- *40 g* feta, crumbled
- *2* spring onions, thinly sliced

---

## Method
1. Beat the eggs with the milk and a pinch of salt and pepper.
2. Wilt the spinach in the oil, then add the egg and pull the cooked edges into the centre.
3. Scatter over the feta and spring onions, fold the omelette and cook for 1 minute more.
